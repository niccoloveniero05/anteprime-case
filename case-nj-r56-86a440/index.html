FAQ

Straight answers.

Still have a question? Call or text Luca at (973) 851-6613.

Do I need to make any repairs?

No. We buy the house as-is. Old kitchen, leaky roof, foundation issues: you don't fix anything.

Do I have to clean out the house first?

No. Leave anything you don't want to keep: furniture, boxes, trash, the stuff in the attic. Take what matters to you and we'll take care of the rest.

Are there fees or commissions?

We don't charge agent commissions when you sell directly to us. We can also cover agreed-upon closing costs, which will be clearly outlined in your written offer. Existing mortgages, liens, unpaid property taxes and other obligations are handled through the closing process.

How fast can we close?

On your timeline. We can move fast if you're in a hurry, or take our time if you need to sort things out first. The one thing that can slow us down is legal paperwork, like a lien or estate papers on an inherited house.

Am I obligated to accept your offer?

Not at all. Our cash offers are free and come with no obligation. You can review the offer and decide whether it's right for you.
