Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

This Privacy Policy explains how [LEGAL BUSINESS NAME] (“Cash the House”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses and protects your information when you visit cashthehouse.com or contact us. We buy houses in New Jersey, and we only ask for the information we need to talk with you about your property.

Information we collect

Information you give us. When you fill out our form or contact us, we collect your name, phone number, the property address, when you would like to sell, how you heard about us, and anything else you choose to tell us. If you call or text us, we receive your phone number and the content of your messages.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, our hosting provider may record basic technical information, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit, to keep the website running and secure. We do not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels on this website.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your request and contact you about your property.
  • To review the property and prepare a cash offer.
  • To arrange and complete the sale, if you decide to move forward.
  • To keep records of our conversations and meet our legal obligations.
  • To keep our website secure and working properly.

Calls and text messages

When you submit our form, you agree that we may contact you by phone, text message or email about your property. Message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP to any text message to stop receiving texts, or simply tell us you no longer want to be contacted. Agreeing to be contacted is not a condition of selling your house to us. We do not share your mobile number or your consent to receive text messages with third parties for their own marketing.

How we share your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only:

  • with service providers that help us run our business, such as website hosting, the service that delivers our form to our email, and our email provider, who may use it only to provide their services to us;
  • with title companies, attorneys and other parties involved in the sale, if you decide to move forward;
  • when required by law, or to protect our rights and the safety of others.

How long we keep it

We keep your information as long as we need it for the purposes above, including records we must keep for legal, tax or accounting reasons. If you ask us to delete it, we will, unless we are required to keep it.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we have about you, or to stop contacting you. Depending on where you live, including New Jersey, you may have additional rights under state privacy laws. To make a request, contact us using the details below. We will not treat you differently for making a request.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. However, no method of sending or storing information over the internet is completely secure.

Children

This website is meant for adults who own or manage property. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Other websites

Our website links to our Instagram and TikTok pages. Those websites have their own privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
